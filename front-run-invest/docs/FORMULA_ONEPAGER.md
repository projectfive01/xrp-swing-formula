# Front Run Invest — Formula One-Pager

**Status:** Active research framework  
**Style:** Craig Percoco-inspired (Bitcoin anchor → higher-beta activity names)

---

## Philosophy

1. Bitcoin leads.
2. Capital then rotates further out the risk curve.
3. Own names that already show **real activity** while the market is quiet.
4. When Bitcoin runs, higher-beta names can do the heavy lifting.

This is research + positioning, not day trading.

---

## BASE Rules

### 1. Market Regime Preference
Prefer building positions while Bitcoin is consolidating or quiet — not after a vertical blow-off.

### 2. Candidate Filter (need ≥ 2 of 3)
- **Activity Signal:** Outlier volume, fees, revenue, or open interest while broader market is muted
- **Relative Strength:** Holding up better than most alts
- **Beta Profile:** Estimated medium-to-high beta vs Bitcoin (roughly 2x–5x)

### 3. Thesis Requirement
Every position needs a clear 1–2 sentence thesis.

Example:  
> “This protocol is already generating real usage/fees and sits further out on the risk curve, so it should outperform Bitcoin on the next sustained risk-on move.”

### 4. Position Sizing
- Seed: 0.5–1.5% of invest capital
- Core: 1.5–3.0% of invest capital
- Max per name: ~3–4%
- Total Front Run book: target 15–25% of invest capital

### 5. Entry
Scale in on strength or controlled pullbacks after activity is already proven. Avoid chasing parabolic extensions.

### 6. Invalidation / Exit
Exit or heavily trim when:
- On-chain activity collapses and does not recover
- Competitive position is clearly lost
- Thesis is broken
- Major structural risk-off that invalidates the beta assumption

Targets are secondary. Primary exit = thesis break.

### 7. Review Cadence
Weekly or bi-weekly review of every name (activity, relative strength, thesis health).

---

## Supporting Tools

- Beta vs Bitcoin (estimated or statistical)
- Rolling alpha stability
- On-chain metrics: fees, volume, OI, active users, revenue/TVL

---

## Explicitly Forbidden

- Tight 1R day-trading stops on these positions
- Turning investment theses into short-term trades
- Adding size only because price is down
- Concentrating the entire book in the highest-beta names

---

## One-Line Summary

> Own the names that are already working quietly, sized modestly, so that when Bitcoin leads the next expansion, the higher-beta names can do the heavy lifting.
