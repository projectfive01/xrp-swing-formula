# Front Run Invest

Longer-term research and positioning system.

**Style:** Craig Percoco-inspired  
**Anchor:** Bitcoin  
**Goal:** Own names that already show real activity while the market is quiet, so higher beta can work when risk-on returns.

---

## Core Idea

1. Bitcoin leads.
2. Capital rotates further out the risk curve.
3. We want protocols that are already generating fees, volume, or open interest **before** the broad market turns.

This is **not** day trading. Positions are thesis-based and held for weeks to months unless the thesis breaks.

---

## Selection Criteria (need at least 2 of 3)

- **Activity Signal** — outlier volume, fees, revenue, or OI while broader market is muted
- **Relative Strength** — holding up better than most alts
- **Beta Profile** — estimated medium-to-high beta vs Bitcoin (roughly 2x–5x range)

---

## Position Sizing

| Stage | Allocation |
|-------|------------|
| Seed | 0.5–1.5% of invest capital |
| Core | 1.5–3.0% of invest capital |
| Max per name | ~3–4% |
| Total book | Target 15–25% of invest capital |

---

## Exit Rules

Exit or heavily trim when:
- On-chain activity collapses and does not recover
- Competitive position is clearly lost
- Thesis is broken
- Major structural risk-off that invalidates the beta assumption

No tight 1R day-trading stops on these positions.

---

## Review Cadence

- Weekly or bi-weekly review of every name
- Re-check activity, relative strength, and thesis health
