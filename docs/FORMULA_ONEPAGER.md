# Formula one-pager

## Setup
XRP >=20% in <=10 days and daily RSI >=75 (exhaustion).

## Entry
First >=10% pullback from local high while holding 200-day zone.
Do not buy the spike. Do not require full RSI cool-off.

## Exit
- Stop = 1R (max loss)
- Take Profit 1 = 3R (scale)
- Take Profit 2 = 5R
- Time fallback: 7-10 trading days or daily close under 200-day

## Size (Standard Unit)
**Fixed unit = $2,000 notional**
- Risk per unit = 1R
- Same unit size used for XRP swing and SOL day trades

## Catalyst
-1 / 0 / +1 weight only.

## Action
Load bar 0-100%. BUY only at 100%.
